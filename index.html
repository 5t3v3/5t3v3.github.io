I work in cybersecurity with a simple mindset: understand how something can be broken, then figure out how to make it harder to break.

My work spans security engineering, offensive security, application security, vulnerability research and building security solutions. I enjoy going beyond the obvious, digging into systems, finding weaknesses, understanding attack paths, and turning findings into practical improvements.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of offensive security, real-world attacks, automation and emerging technology. I like building things. I like breaking things. And I like understanding why they break.

Always learning. Always experimenting. Always looking for what others missed.
